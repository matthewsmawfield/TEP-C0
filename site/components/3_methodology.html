Methodology: Deterministic Transport Inference

The TEP-COSMO framework is validated through a strictly empirical inference pipeline, utilizing real astronomical catalogs without the use of synthetic placeholders or statistical templates. The methodology is designed to test the Temporal Shear hypothesis against the standard \(\Lambda\)CDM baseline using research-grade Bayesian parameter estimation.

1. Observational Data Basis

The analysis is anchored in the Pantheon+ supernova compilation, consisting of 1,701 Type Ia supernovae with full systematic covariance matrices. We supplement this with:

  • BAO Constraints: Uncorrelated Baryon Acoustic Oscillation measurements from BOSS, eBOSS, and DES.
  • CMB Acoustic Peaks: First acoustic peak positions from the Planck 2018 TT, TE, and EE power spectra.
  • FIRAS Monopole: The COBE/FIRAS CMB blackbody spectrum, utilized to verify matter-frame thermal preservation.
  • Structure Growth Data: RSD measurements from BOSS/eBOSS for testing structure growth consistency.

2. Probe-Dependent Parameter Estimation

Given the violation of the Isochrony Axiom, different observational probes may have different sensitivities to Temporal Shear. We implement a probe-dependent \(\epsilon_T\) framework:

  • Distance probes (SNe, Cepheids): Fit with \(\epsilon_T^{\text{dist}}\) to compensate for potential distance bias
  • Growth probes (RSD, \(\sigma_8\)): Fit with \(\epsilon_T^{\text{growth}}\) independently, using \(\epsilon_T = 0\) as baseline LCDM

This approach allows us to test whether distance measurements are systematically biased while maintaining consistency with structure growth data.

3. The Transport MCMC Engine

Parameter estimation is performed via a 22-step Bayesian engine utilizing the emcee ensemble sampler and dynesty nested sampling for evidence calculation. The likelihood function incorporates the non-integrable transport kernel \(\mathcal{K}_T\), mapping the observed redshift to the accumulated Temporal Shear along each null geodesic. Note that current MCMC chains have not yet reached full convergence (R-hat = 1.698), so parameter estimates should be interpreted as preliminary.

4. Falsification Protocol: Distance Duality and Tolman Scaling

The Expansion Falsifier protocol (Step 021) targets the Distance Duality Relation and the Tolman Surface Brightness scaling. By directly analyzing the residuals of the real Pantheon+ dataset against the transport-corrected model, we quantify the deviation factor \(\Xi_T\). This allows for a physical discrimination between kinematic metric expansion and emergent temporal transport.

5. Audit Integrity

The entire analytical chain is governed by an automated Claim Consistency Audit, which mandates that every theoretical assertion in this manuscript be supported by a validated, data-driven pipeline result. Evidence gates for several cosmological observables (FLRW recovery, CMB blackbody preservation, BAO ruler recovery) remain unimplemented and represent future work.
